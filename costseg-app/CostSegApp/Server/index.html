What is Cost Segregation?

Instead of depreciating a whole building over 27.5 or 39 years, a cost segregation study splits out the parts that wear out faster. Those parts get shorter lives, which means bigger deductions sooner. Land is never depreciated!

Example Study

ItemAmount
Purchase price$500,000
Less: land (20%)($100,000)
Depreciable basis$400,000
Reclassified to 5/7/15-year (25%)$100,000
Stays 27.5-year$300,000

Without a study: about $14,545/yr ($400,000 ÷ 27.5).
With a study: the $100,000 is written off over 5-15 years instead, pulling deductions forward.
